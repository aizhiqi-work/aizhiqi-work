# Zhiqi Ai

**Ph.D. Student · Shanghai University**

[**Academic Website ↗**](https://aizhiqi-work.github.io/) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=xQsSstkAAAAJ&hl=en) · [CV & Background](https://aizhiqi-work.github.io/cv/) · [Email](mailto:aizhiqi-work@shu.edu.cn)

I work on **speech and audio intelligence**, spanning wake-word detection, speaker recognition, speech synthesis, and speech language models.

My current focus is **spatial audio simulation and sim-to-real (Sim2Real) transfer**, exploring how acoustic simulation can support audio perception in real-world environments.

> Visit my **[academic website](https://aizhiqi-work.github.io/)** for publications, research projects, education, awards, and CV.
